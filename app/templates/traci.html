TRACI 2.2 characterization factors

Search substances by name or CAS number. Each row describes the estimated environmental or human-health impact associated with releasing one kilogram of a substance into the specified environment.

How to read this table
  • CAS registry number is a standard identifier for a chemical substance.
  • Factor columns show impact per kilogram of substance for the release type and environment in the column heading. These are characterization factors, not measured facility emissions.
  • Equivalent (eq) expresses an impact relative to a reference substance, such as CO2 for climate change.
  • CTUeco is the ecotoxicity factor unit. CTUcancer and CTUnoncancer are human-health factor units.
  • Data flags describe the source factor's data category. A dash means the source workbook has no value.
  • Hover over a column heading to see its original TRACI workbook label.
